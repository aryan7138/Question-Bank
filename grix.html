02 Info / FAQ

Common questions.

Answers in one place Still stuck? Write us.
How long does delivery take?
Inside Dhaka: 1–2 working days. Outside Dhaka but within Bangladesh: 2–4 working days. We ship to all 64 districts and send you a tracking number as soon as your order leaves our studio.
Do you offer customisation?
Yes — it's the reason we exist. Every piece can be customised with initials, sleeve length, colour, or a small embroidery detail. Message us on WhatsApp before ordering and we'll walk you through the options.
What's your return policy?
If it doesn't fit or isn't what you expected, send it back within 7 days of delivery — unworn, in its original packaging — and we'll refund or exchange it. Custom-embroidered pieces are final sale, since they're made specifically for you.
Are your pieces unisex?
Every piece is designed unisex and cut to work on any body. Our size chart is generous — if you're between sizes, we recommend sizing down for a cleaner fit or sizing up for something roomier.
How do I pay?
We accept bKash, Nagad, and cash on delivery inside Bangladesh. For international orders, reach out on WhatsApp and we'll arrange a payment method that works for you.
Where are you based?
Dhaka, Bangladesh. Everything is designed, cut, and packed here — we don't outsource. If you're nearby and want to see the pieces in person, message us and we can arrange a studio visit.
